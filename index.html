About Me

My Journey

I started building websites out of pure passion for technology and design. Over the years, I've developed and launched multiple live digital platforms — spanning farm-fresh Himalayan e-commerce stores, B2B diamond sourcing portals, practical digital skills learning platforms, music streaming networks, and AI chatbot automation suites.

I manage the entire digital lifecycle from end to end: architecture, frontend development, WooCommerce & Shopify backend integration, SEO ranking, and Google/Meta performance advertising. My focus is always on building experiences that convert visitors into loyal clients.

My philosophy is simple: understand the core business objective, engineer clean and scalable code, and ensure a flawless user experience across every screen size.
